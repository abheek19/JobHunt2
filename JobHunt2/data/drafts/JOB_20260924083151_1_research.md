# Research Brief: Lemon.io – Senior AI Engineer

## 1. Company Overview
* **Company:** Lemon.io (formerly Coding Ninjas)
* **Business Model:** A vetted talent marketplace connecting top-tier freelance engineers (primarily from CEE, LATAM, South Asia/India) with vetted startups across the US and Western Europe.
* **Key Stats:** Over $11M+ paid out to developers on the platform. Known for a developer-first ethos, fast matching processes (often within 48–72 hours after joining the network), and handling client management/payments on behalf of contractors.
* **Culture & Vetting:** Highly selective vetting process (screening English communication, hard skills test/portfolio review, and live technical interviews).

---

## 2. Role Context & Expectations
* **Position:** Senior AI Engineer (Remote - India focus)
* **Model:** Freelance / Dedicated Contracting (full-time or part-time hourly engagements with matching startups).
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  * Build end-to-end AI/ML systems, integrate LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source models like Llama), implement RAG architectures, and fine-tune models for client startups.
  * Direct communication with founding teams and CTOs at fast-paced US/EU startups.
  * Rapid prototyping, clean architecture, and standard software engineering practices (Python, LangChain/LlamaIndex, PyTorch, Cloud Deployment).

---

## 3. Strategic Insights & Application Strategy

### A. Understanding the Vetting Pipeline
Unlike traditional full-time employment where you apply to a single company's HR department, applying to **Lemon.io** means passing their internal vetting process to enter their active talent pool:
1. **Initial Screening / English Proficiency:** Strong written and spoken English is mandatory, as you will interact directly with US/EU startup founders.
2. **Technical Assessment:** Hands-on coding/AI architecture test and technical interview.
3. **Matching:** Once accepted, Lemon.io's matchmakers actively pitch your profile to relevant client projects.

### B. Resume & Application Tailoring Tips
* **Highlight Impact & Metrics:** Emphasize real-world AI applications (e.g., *"Reduced latency of RAG pipeline by 40% using vector indexing"* or *"Integrated custom LLM agents boosting user retention by 25%"*).
* **Stack Focus:** Prominently showcase Python, OpenAI API, Anthropic, LangChain/LlamaIndex, Pinecone/Qdrant/Milvus, PyTorch, Docker, AWS/GCP.
* **Startup-Ready Mindset:** Highlight any past experience working with remote international teams, agile development, or direct client communication.
